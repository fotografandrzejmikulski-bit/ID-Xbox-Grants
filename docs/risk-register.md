# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| R-01 | Funding scope differs from planning assumption | Medium | High | Verify current DAP terms before submission; maintain minimum/full funding variants | Producer |
| R-02 | Project scope too large for 12-month target | Medium | High | Lock core loop; stage non-critical features; enforce milestone gates | Producer / Game Director |
| R-03 | Rhythm mechanic is difficult to make accessible | High | High | Separate timing difficulty from narrative progression; provide alternate cues and configurable timing | Design / Accessibility |
| R-04 | Performance target missed on target hardware | Medium | High | Profile early; maintain scene budgets; gate content on measured performance | Engineering |
| R-05 | Platform service assumptions prove incorrect | Medium | High | Build a current platform capability matrix before implementation; avoid hard-coding unverified assumptions | Technical Lead |
| R-06 | Certification/submission defect discovered late | Medium | High | Continuous platform QA; release-readiness reviews from mid-production | QA Lead |
| R-07 | Accessibility issues discovered near release | Medium | High | Accessibility testing from Vertical Slice; recurring external playtests | Accessibility Lead |
| R-08 | Unsupported claim weakens grant credibility | High | High | Use verification matrix; remove unsourced numeric/program claims | Producer |
| R-09 | Marketing plan depends on undocumented storefront behavior | Medium | Medium | Treat store visibility assumptions as hypotheses; use measurable campaign KPIs | Marketing |
| R-10 | Key-person dependency | Medium | High | Document systems; cross-train owners; maintain recovery plans | Production |
| R-11 | IP / legal documentation incomplete | Low/Medium | High | Maintain chain-of-title and corporate documentation package | Legal / Producer |
| R-12 | Build/release tooling causes packaging delays | Medium | Medium | Establish packaging CI and repeatable release checklist early | Build Engineer |

## Risk gates

No milestone should be considered complete when a high-impact unresolved risk is merely deferred without an owner, mitigation and due date.
