# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Positioning

**Core promise:** a narrative action-RPG in which music is the player's primary instrument of agency.

The marketing should lead with the mechanic and player experience, then explain the cultural and narrative context. Avoid presenting the title as a generic "diversity project"; the strongest pitch is a distinctive game whose perspective is inseparable from its mechanics and world.

## Audience pillars

### Primary

Players interested in narrative-driven action games, atmospheric worlds and unusual core mechanics.

### Secondary

Players attracted to rhythm, puzzle and experimental gameplay systems; audiences interested in folklore-inspired worlds and culturally specific storytelling.

### Accessibility audience

Players who value configurable timing, visual/audio alternatives, flexible input and accessible UI. Accessibility features should be communicated precisely rather than treated as marketing promises without implementation evidence.

## Pre-launch milestones

| Window | Objective | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| T-9 to T-6 months | Establish identity and mechanic | Trailer engagement, wishlists, qualified press interest |
| T-6 to T-3 months | Demonstrate depth | Gameplay completion rate, wishlist growth, creator coverage |
| T-3 to T-1 month | Convert awareness to intent | Wishlist velocity, demo/build feedback, mailing/community growth |
| Launch | Maximize qualified reach | Conversion, review quality, retention |
| Post-launch | Sustain discovery | Update cadence, earned media, community activity |

## Store strategy

All store claims must be generated from the verified platform capability matrix. Avoid unsupported claims such as:

- guaranteed placement in a specific storefront section,
- guaranteed algorithmic amplification,
- guaranteed sales uplift from a specific launch weekday,
- guaranteed Game Pass placement.

## Game Pass / commercial deals

Treat Game Pass and similar commercial arrangements as business-development opportunities subject to Microsoft review and negotiation. They should not be used as assumed grant benefits or guaranteed revenue in the base-case forecast.

## Launch window

Select the launch date after reviewing platform timing, competing releases, production readiness and audience behavior. The final application should present the date as a planning choice, not as a guaranteed storefront optimization mechanism.

## Creative assets

Prepare a compact press package:

- hero key art,
- concise game description,
- 30-second pitch video,
- gameplay trailer,
- screenshots,
- accessibility feature list,
- team bio,
- fact sheet,
- contact details.

Every claim in the press kit should match the shipped or explicitly planned product state.
